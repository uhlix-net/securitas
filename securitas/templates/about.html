About Securitas

Version {{ version }}{% if cfg.cluster_name %} · {{ cfg.cluster_name }}{% endif %}

Securitas reports vulnerabilities and compliance for Kubernetes, Linux and Windows. It scans on a schedule or on demand, ranks CVEs by the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog and EPSS scores, checks hosts against CIS benchmarks, keeps every scan in its history, and serves the results as web pages and PDFs.

Vulnerability scansKubernetes: Trivy Operator's vulnerability reports. Linux: pending security advisories (dnf updateinfo) over SSH. Windows: OS build compared with Microsoft's security release data, over WinRM. Nothing is installed or changed on the scanned systems.

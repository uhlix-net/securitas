Windows (WinRM)

The account Securitas signs in as over WinRM. It needs no admin rights: add it to Remote Management Users and give it WMI Enable Account and Remote Enable on root\cimv2 on each server. Use DOMAIN\user or user@domain. The password must match the account's password in the directory.

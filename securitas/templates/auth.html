Group to role mappings

LDAP and single sign-on users get the highest role mapped from their groups, checked at each sign-in. Users without a mapped group cannot sign in unless an admin gives them a fixed role on the Users page. Group names are matched without regard to case; for LDAP the group's CN is used.
